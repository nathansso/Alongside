How it works
01 · Log
Say how today went
Type it or speak it — a sentence is enough. Your words are stored exactly as you said them and quoted back to you, never rewritten.
02 · Visualize
See what connects to what
Check-ins link to medications parsed from your labels. When two things interact, the connection between them is drawn — and you can follow it hop by hop.
03 · Advocate
Walk in with a page
Concerns collect into one printable page of questions, each with its date and source. It empties as things get answered.
