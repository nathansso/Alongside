myCancerPal  /  UI direction proposal

A quiet clinical document, not a dashboard.

The current build already has the hard part right — everything on screen is traceable to something the patient said or a label that was parsed. The interface just doesn't yet look like it can be trusted or carried into an exam room. This direction keeps the structure and rebuilds the surface: paper-toned, one column, generous type, provenance treated as a first-class element rather than fine print.

Readable at arm's length
17px body minimum. Chemo brain, neuropathy, and waiting rooms are the design constraint.
Provenance is the product
Every claim shows its two sources in monospace. Nothing appears without a receipt.
Colour carries meaning only
Two accents, each with one job. No gradients, no decorative colour, no glow.
One thing per screen
Check in. Read the page. Print the page. Three verbs, three tabs.
